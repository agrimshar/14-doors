The three numbers the game runs on, drawn as instruments with hairlines and diamonds.

- **The tide** is a beam with a level zone in the middle 20 percent. The marker is `paper` when level and `warn` when off. **You provide** a value from -1 to 1. The word under it reads level, running high or running low.
- **The well** is the ring around the breathing light, drawn clockwise in `paper` over `faint`. **You provide** a value from 0 to 1. Holding the light grows it by up to 35 percent; releasing refills the well.
- **Lights carried** is five diamonds. An empty one is outlined in `dim`. A carried one fills with its door's `-a` colour and glows.
- Labels use the `label` style. Readouts use display italic at 18px, because the readout is the narrator's reading of the instrument.
