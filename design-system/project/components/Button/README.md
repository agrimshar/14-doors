Square, uppercase mono buttons that say exactly what happens next.

- **Outline** is the default action on a live world. **Filled** (`paper` with `ink` text) is the single way forward on a text screen. **Morning** (`dawn`) appears once in the game, when five lights are carried and the player is level. **Link** is for quiet exits such as Leave early and the sound toggle.
- **You provide:** the label, in the `action` style. Two to four words, a verb first.
- Corners are `radius-square`. One filled button per screen at most.
- Disabled buttons stay visible at 32 percent so the player can see what they are working toward.
